# PlushCraft
PlushCraft brings adorable, collectible plushies to Minecraft! Craft and place 10 cute plushies inspired by your favorite mobs, including Creepers, Bees, Axolotls, and more. Decorate your home, build a plushie collection, and make your world feel extra cozy! With custom 3D models, unique textures, and simple crafting recipes, PlushCraft adds charm!
